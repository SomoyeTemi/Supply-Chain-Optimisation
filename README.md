# Optimizing Supply Chain Operations for Uniquexcel

### An Excel-Based Business Analysis Case Study

## 1. Project Overview

Efficient supply chain management is essential for controlling operational costs, ensuring timely deliveries, maintaining product quality, and improving profitability.

This project examines Uniquexcel's supply chain operations, focusing on shipping costs, transportation delays, supplier performance, manufacturing lead times, product defects, and profitability.

Using Microsoft Excel, I analysed supply chain data to identify operational inefficiencies, evaluate performance trends, and develop actionable business recommendations to support better decision-making.

## 2. Problem Statement

Uniquexcel faces several supply chain challenges that could negatively affect operational efficiency, profitability, and customer satisfaction.

The major business challenges identified include:

- **Stockouts:** Product availability issues that could lead to lost sales and customer dissatisfaction.
- **Defective Products:** Quality issues that could increase operational costs and affect customer satisfaction.
- **Delivery Delays:** Transportation inefficiencies that could disrupt supply chain operations.
- **Increasing Shipping and Manufacturing Costs:** Rising operational expenses that could reduce overall profitability.

These challenges highlight the need to evaluate supplier performance, transportation costs, manufacturing efficiency, and product profitability to identify opportunities for improvement.

## 3. Project Objectives

The primary objective was to analyse Uniquexcel's supply chain operations and identify opportunities to improve efficiency and profitability.

The analysis aimed to:

1. Identify shipping routes with the highest and lowest costs.
2. Evaluate supplier performance based on transportation delays.
3. Examine manufacturing lead times and defect rates across suppliers.
4. Identify high-performing and low-performing products based on profitability.
5. Analyse shipping costs across transportation modes.
6. Develop actionable business recommendations to improve supply chain operations.

## 4. Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel** was the only tool used for this project.

Excel was used for data analysis, visualisation, and dashboard creation to communicate supply chain performance and business insights.

## 6. Data Analysis and Key Findings

### 6.1 Shipping Cost Analysis

**Business Question:** Which shipping routes incur the highest costs, and where can potential savings be identified?

**Findings:**

- Route A recorded the highest shipping cost.
- Route B had the second-highest shipping cost.
- Route C recorded the lowest shipping cost.

**Business Insight:**

Route C presents a potential opportunity for reducing shipping expenses. However, delivery distances, shipment volumes, and delivery times should be compared before changing routes.

### 6.2 Supplier Performance and Transportation Delays

**Business Question:** Which suppliers experience the highest transportation delays?

**Findings:**

- Supplier 3 recorded the highest transportation delays.
- Supplier 4 recorded the lowest transportation delays.

**Business Insight:**

Supplier 3's transportation delays could affect delivery reliability and customer satisfaction.

Investigating the causes of these delays could help Uniquexcel improve supplier coordination and delivery efficiency.

### 6.3 Manufacturing Lead Times and Product Defects

**Business Question:** How do manufacturing lead times and product defect rates vary across suppliers?

**Findings:**

- Supplier 4 recorded the longest manufacturing lead time.
- Supplier 2 recorded the shortest manufacturing lead time.
- Product defect rates varied across suppliers.

**Business Insight:**

Differences in manufacturing lead times highlight opportunities to review supplier efficiency and production processes.

Reducing unnecessary manufacturing delays and improving quality control could support better product availability and operational performance.

### 6.4 Product Profitability Analysis

**Business Question:** Which products contribute the most to profitability, and which require further review?

**Findings:**

- Product profitability varied across SKUs.
- SKU198 recorded the highest profit among the products shown in one profitability chart.
- The presentation's business recommendations highlighted SKU347 and SKU177 as high-performing products.
- SKU61 was identified as a product requiring further profitability review.

**Business Insight:**

Understanding product-level profitability can help Uniquexcel make informed decisions about inventory prioritisation, production planning, and cost management.

*The presentation uses different SKU groups across its profitability charts. Product rankings should be verified against the Excel workbook before making an overall profitability ranking.*

### 6.5 Transportation Mode and Shipping Costs

**Business Question:** How are total shipping costs distributed across transportation modes?

**Findings:**

- Air transportation accounted for approximately 36% of total shipping costs.
- Road transportation accounted for approximately 33%.
- Rail transportation accounted for approximately 31%.

**Business Insight:**

Air transportation represented the largest share of total shipping costs.

However, this does not necessarily mean that air is the most expensive transportation method for equivalent shipments. Further analysis of shipment volumes, distances, and delivery requirements would support better decisions.

## 7. Business Recommendations

### 7.1 Reduce Shipping Costs by Prioritising Route C

**Finding:** Route A recorded the highest shipping cost, while Route C recorded the lowest.

**Recommendation:**

Uniquexcel should consider using Route C instead of Route A for suitable deliveries.

Before implementing this change, the company should compare delivery distances, shipment volumes, and delivery times to ensure that the alternative route provides genuine cost savings.

### 7.2 Reduce Transportation Delays by Reviewing Supplier 3

**Finding:** Supplier 3 recorded the highest transportation delays, while Supplier 4 recorded the lowest.

**Recommendation:**

Uniquexcel should investigate the factors contributing to Supplier 3's delays and review the delivery practices of Supplier 4.

If Supplier 3 cannot improve its performance, the company could consider reallocating suitable orders to alternative suppliers, provided they meet operational requirements.

### 7.3 Improve Manufacturing Efficiency by Reviewing Supplier 4

**Finding:** Supplier 4 recorded the longest manufacturing lead time, while Supplier 2 recorded the shortest.

**Recommendation:**

Uniquexcel should investigate why Supplier 4 requires more time to manufacture products.

The company could also evaluate Supplier 2 for suitable production orders, provided it meets the required quality, production capacity, and cost standards.

### 7.4 Increase Product Profitability

**Finding:** Product profitability varies across SKUs, with some products generating higher profits than others.

**Recommendation:**

Uniquexcel should prioritise high-performing products such as SKU347 and SKU177 while reviewing lower-performing products such as SKU61, as identified in the presentation.

The company should investigate whether manufacturing or shipping cost reductions could improve the profitability of lower-performing products.

### 7.5 Improve Transportation Cost Efficiency

**Finding:** Air transportation accounted for approximately 36% of total shipping costs, followed by road at 33% and rail at 31%.

**Recommendation:**

Uniquexcel should investigate whether some non-urgent deliveries currently transported by air could be moved to road or rail.

Before making changes, the company should compare costs per shipment, delivery requirements, distances, and service reliability.

## 8. Conclusion

The analysis identified opportunities for Uniquexcel to improve supply chain efficiency by addressing shipping costs, supplier delays, manufacturing lead times, and product profitability.

The findings suggest that selecting cost-effective shipping routes, improving supplier performance, reviewing manufacturing processes, and prioritising profitable products could support better operational decisions.

By implementing suitable recommendations, Uniquexcel could potentially reduce unnecessary expenses, improve delivery reliability, and strengthen overall supply chain performance.
